ScholarMatchGitHub

Don't find scholarships. Let scholarships find you.

Upload a resume or fill out a quick profile. ScholarMatch scores every scholarship against it, shows the ones that fit, and tracks your applications.

Try the matching

Change the profile and the ranking changes. The scholarships are made up and the scoring is simplified, but the weights are the engine's own.

    How it matches

    Filter
    Scholarships you aren't eligible for are removed, with the reason: GPA, academic level, location, program or need.
    Score
    GPA counts for 40%, financial need 25%, program 20%, location 10% and deadline 5%.
    Understand
    Embeddings compare your qualities with what each scholarship looks for. The final score is 60% points and 40% this fit.
    Advise
    Gemini reads your resume, answers questions about essays and eligibility, and builds a roadmap of what to improve.
    Track
    A dashboard keeps your deadlines and application statuses in one place.

    Built with

    React, Tailwind, FastAPI, Supabase, Auth0, Google Gemini, Cloudinary and Docker. Setup steps are in the README.
