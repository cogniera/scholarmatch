ScholarMatch

CodeDevpost

ScholarMatch reads a student's resume or profile, scores every scholarship against it, and keeps track of the applications.

Demo

    Made up scholarships, with simplified scoring that uses the engine's real weights.

    Matching

    Filter
    Scholarships a student is not eligible for are removed, with the reason.
    Score
    GPA counts for 40%, financial need for 25%, program for 20%, location for 10% and the deadline for 5%.
    Fit
    Embeddings compare the student's qualities with what each scholarship looks for. The final score is 60% points and 40% fit.
    Advice
    Gemini reads the resume, answers questions about essays and eligibility, and suggests what to improve.
    Tracking
    One dashboard holds every deadline and application status.

    Stack

    React, Tailwind, FastAPI, Supabase, Auth0, Google Gemini, Cloudinary and Docker. Setup steps are in the README.
